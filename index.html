FAQ

Common questions.

How much does each service cost?

Warrior to Mythical Honor (up to 49 stars) costs ₱15 per game. Mythical Glory (50–75 stars) and 10-man service cost ₱20 per game. Classic Bug costs ₱10 per game.

How many RGBOT games can I book each day?

The RGBOT daily limit is five games. Message us to check available slots.

How do I book a service?

Message us on Messenger with your current rank, star count, chosen service, and number of games. We will confirm availability, the total price, and your schedule.

Do you offer pilot services?

Yes. Send your target hero and rank through Messenger so we can discuss the price and schedule.

Can I request more than 75 stars?

The listed Mythical Glory service covers 50 to 75 stars. Message us if your target is outside this range.

Where can I ask other questions?

Use the Messenger link below to send your question. You can also visit our Facebook and TikTok pages.
